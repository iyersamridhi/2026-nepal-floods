2. Privacy & data (Nepal / personal data)

We designed this site so that we do not collect, receive, transmit to our servers, or store personal data about visitors or people named in reports.

  • No accounts, no databases of missing persons on our side. We do not operate a missing-person registry.
  • Report helper is device-local. Details you type on the Report page stay in your browser until you choose to send them yourself via WhatsApp, email, or by copying text. We do not upload that form to our servers.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers are intentionally embedded by us.
  • Language preference only. Your browser may store a language choice (lang) in local storage so the site can remember EN/ने. That is not identity or case data.
  • Static hosting. Like most websites, the host or CDN you use to reach this page may process standard technical logs (for example IP address, browser type, request time) under their own policies. We do not use those logs to identify individuals or build case files.
  • Third-party destinations. When you open Nepal Police, WhatsApp, email, Twitter/X, or government sites, those services process data under their terms and applicable law — not ours.

We recognise that Nepal has (and continues to develop) privacy and personal-data protections. This notice is meant to make our limited role clear: we are an information-routing aid, not a data controller of missing-person or family data. If you believe any content on this static site should be corrected or removed, use the Feedback page.

  • हाम्रो तर्फबाट खाता वा हराएको व्यक्तिको डाटाबेस छैन।
  • रिपोर्ट सहायक तपाईंको उपकरणमै रहन्छ। फारममा लेखेको विवरण ब्राउजरमै रहन्छ; तपाईं आफैं WhatsApp, इमेल वा कपी गरेर पठाउनुहुन्छ। हामी त्यो फारम आफ्नो सर्भरमा अपलोड गर्दैनौं।
  • हामीले जानाजानी एनालिटिक्स वा विज्ञापन ट्र्याकर राखेका छैनौं।

३. प्रयोगका सर्तहरू

  1. मानवीय प्रयोग। आधिकारिक च्यानल खोज्न र अधिकारीलाई सन्देश तयार गर्न प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्। दुर्व्यवहार, ठगी, आधिकारिक पहिचान नक्कली बनाउने वा झूटो दाबी फैलाउन प्रयोग नगर्नुहोस्।
  2. आधिकारिक सम्बन्ध छैन। सरकारी नाम, फोन वा लिङ्क देखाउँदैमा साझेदारी वा अनुमति मानिँदैन।
  3. वारेन्टी छैन। सामग्री “जस्ताको तस्तै” उपलब्ध छ।
  4. दायित्व सीमा। कानुनले अनुमति दिएसम्म, यस सूचनामा भर पर्दा हुने निर्णय/नतिजाका लागि स्वयंसेवी सञ्चालकहरू जिम्मेवार हुँदैनन्।
  5. बाह्य लिङ्क। लिङ्क गरिएका साइट हाम्रो नियन्त्रणबाहिर छन्।
  6. परिवर्तन। यी सर्तहरू कुनै पनि समय अद्यावधिक हुन सक्छन्।
